ABOUT ME.

I'm a Unity Gameplay Engineer based in Chișinău, six years into building production titles for mobile, AR, VR and PC. Most of my work sits underneath what players see: the gameplay systems, the dependency-injected architecture, and the UI layer other people extend later.

In practice that means composing projects with Zenject, driving flow through UniTask and R3 instead of Update loops, and building UI that survives a redesign without a rewrite. The other half of the job is performance — profiling with Unity's toolset, cutting draw calls, batching, and shrinking memory with Addressables until the build runs on the weakest device in the target list.

I've owned features end to end (a DLC card system, a subtitle system, cross-platform multiplayer sync), worked in small teams where architecture decisions are shared, and maintain my own Unity packages to keep the tooling reusable across projects.
